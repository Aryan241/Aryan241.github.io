I am a second-year B.Tech student in Computer Science and Engineering at the Manipal Institute of Technology, Manipal. I work on 3D computer vision, mostly 3D Gaussian Splatting: how scenes are reconstructed, segmented and edited, and what a trained scene file actually stores.

Research interests
------
- 3D Gaussian Splatting and multi-view reconstruction
- Lifting 2D segmentation masks into 3D scenes
- Privacy and machine unlearning for learned scene representations

Current work
------
**Does redaction really forget?** When a private detail, such as a document on a desk, is edited out of a 3DGS scene so that no rendered view shows it, can it still be read from the scene file? I compare common edits against a model retrained on images without the detail, the reference used in machine unlearning. In a synthetic pilot and on a real phone capture, recolouring and fine-tuning made the renders look clean but left a hidden test pattern readable from the Gaussian parameters; only rebuilding the whole edited region brought it down to the retrained model's level. This is my research proposal for the AAAI-27 Undergraduate Consortium (submitted, decision pending). More on the [research page](/research/).
