I am a second-year B.Tech student in Computer Science and Engineering at the Manipal Institute of Technology, Manipal. I work on 3D computer vision, mostly 3D Gaussian Splatting.

Research interests
------
- Computer vision
- 3D reconstruction
- Trustworthy and privacy-aware AI

Current work
------
My current project asks whether a private detail edited out of a 3D Gaussian Splatting scene can still be recovered from the scene file. It is my research proposal for the AAAI-27 Undergraduate Consortium (decision pending). More on the [research page](/research/).

Elsewhere
------
- Member of [Project MANAS](https://projectmanas.in/), the AI and robotics student team of MAHE, where I mentor junior members in machine learning.
- Team lead in e-Yantra, the robotics competition run by IIT Bombay.
